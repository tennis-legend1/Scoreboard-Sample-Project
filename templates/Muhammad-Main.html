About The Game

Jump into CyberMatch, a fast-paced cyber-memory challenge where players test their speed, memory and cybersecurity knowledge. Match digital safety concepts, answer surprise cyber questions and try to create the highest streak possible.


With clean visuals, sharp animations and escalating difficulty, every round feels like a rapid-fire mission. Players must match faster, react smarter and see how long they can keep their streak alive under pressure.


Winner of multiple industry awards, this critically acclaimed challenge delivers unbeatable value and endless replayability. It is a highly recommended addition to your library that is well worth every single cent.

How To Play

Players begin by flipping cards and searching for matching pairs related to important cybersecurity concepts. Every correct match helps build a streak, but mistakes can slow down your progress.


Throughout the game, unexpected cyber pop-ups appear with questions that test your decision making. Answer correctly to improve your attempt and keep your score high.

Gameplay

CyberMatch combines memory skills with cybersecurity learning. With a timer constantly counting down, every move matters as players attempt to finish faster and beat their previous results.
